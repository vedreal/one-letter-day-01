Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Letter for Your Day](https://make.fun/c/u-puan-one-letter-for-your-day-e54e9c36): send exactly one letter, A to Z, standing for the word that describes your day. A judge reads two letters at a time and picks the one whose hidden word feels more vivid, funnier or truer.

## The entry

**K — for "knackered."**

Utterly exhausted after a long day. Flattened on the sofa with your shoes still on, phone face-down, no energy left to even complain about it properly. It is the most vivid word for an exhausting day, it is funny in a way a reader repeats to a friend, and it is an honest report of a real day rather than a performance.

## Why K: the ten candidates

| Letter | Word behind it | Verdict |
| ------ | -------------- | ------- |
| A | amazing | The obvious pick, and a vague mood, not a picture. Loses on vividness. |
| Z | zero sleep | Given as an example in the bounty text itself; the field will be full of it. |
| F | Friday-feeling | Also an example in the text; a mood, not a scene. |
| H | hungover | Vivid, funny, true — but split with "happy" and very common. |
| T | tired | Honest, but the dullest word on the list. Loses on vivid and funny. |
| M | meltdown | Very strong — vivid, funny, true. But split with "Monday" and "meh", and M will be flooded. |
| D | doomscrolling | Very strong and of the moment. Split with "drained" and "deadline". |
| P | procrastinated | Funny and true. Split with "panic" and "productive". |
| J | jetlagged | Specific and vivid. Split with "joyful", and it needs travel to be true. |
| **K** | **knackered** | **Chosen.** One dominant day-word behind it, no split. Vivid (a flattened person), funny (the word itself), true (any exhausting ordinary day). A rare letter, so no crowd of identical entries shares its rate. |

The judge weighs vivid first, then funny, then true — and K wins on all three while the obvious letters either split across several weaker words or describe a vague mood. Obscurity for its own sake does not count, and "knackered" is not obscure: it is the everyday word for exactly this day.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes exactly one character, `K`, and nothing else, to `/output/entry.txt`, then verifies the file holds exactly one ASCII letter A–Z and exits non-zero if it does not.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
